Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: Slow psychedelic R&B / alternative R&B with ambient soul; bpm is around 65 to 70 in a halftime feel, easing near 60 in the middle movement and dissolving into a slow, free tempo for the final movement. Minor key, hazy and nocturnal.
Global Emotional Progression: One continuous piece in three movements that blend into each other. It opens as a lonely, cool 2 AM comedown — woozy, spacious, resigned — then sinks into a darker, muffled stillness heavy with regret, and finally releases: the haze clears into a tender, hymn-like peace that fades slowly to silence.
Application Scenarios & Imagery: A dim apartment at 2 AM, streetlight through the blinds, a piano heard through the wall, first pale light at the window.
Sonics & Production Profile: Wide stereo with soft edges throughout. The first movement is thick and hazy — smeared, woozy synths, lots of space between phrases. The middle movement sounds underwater: heavily low-pass filtered, short dark reverb, faint tape hiss and room noise. The final movement opens into a vast cathedral reverb, airy and weightless, ending in a long slow fade-out.

Vocal Details
Vocal Gender & Timbre: Singer A (Male), soft, breathy tenor — intimate, fragile, and close.
Vocal Style: In the first movement the voice appears only as faint, drifting ad-libs that trail off into the instrumental space. The bridge is a whispered, close-miked confession over the muffled piano. The outro repeats one tender phrase over and over, slow and hymn-like, with devotional calm.
Harmony/Backing Vocals: Minimal until the outro, where the lead is stacked into layered harmonies above and below — high falsetto layers and a low octave — building a soft, breathy choir.
Vocal FX: Long shimmering reverb on the ad-libs so they dissolve into the haze; the whispered bridge is dry and close with short dark reverb; the outro vocals swim in huge cathedral reverb.

Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle (Primary/Secondary Layering):
Primary: Thick, hazy synth pads and a deep, warm electric bass carry the opening movement; a sparse, repeated piano/Rhodes chord figure — heavily low-pass filtered, as if heard through a wall — takes over as the anchor of the middle movement and lingers into the final one.
Secondary: A clean, warm electric guitar rises for an unhurried solo of bluesy bends and slides with light shimmer reverb, leaving long silences between phrases. In the final movement a hymn-like string section swells in with warm organ pads, replacing the rhythm section entirely.
Groove & Foundation Progression: Soft, distant halftime drums and deep bass hold the first movement at a slow drift. Into the middle movement the kick turns muffled and the sub-bass deepens, then drops out altogether, leaving the filtered piano alone with tape hiss. No drums return: the outro floats on strings, organ, and voices in free time, fading slowly to nothing.
Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: Woozy pitch-smeared synth movement across the first movement; faint tape hiss and room noise through the middle; a gradual low-pass filter opening as the strings swell, like a door opening onto morning; a very long final fade.
